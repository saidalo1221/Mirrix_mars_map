# NASA Space Apps 2026 submission checklist

## Verified event information

- Use the **Project** tab on the team page. The global site opens project submissions Saturday, November 14 at 09:00 and closes Sunday, November 15 at 23:59 local time. The global event page lists a maximum of six participants per team. [Official 2026 event page](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/)
- The Global Organizing Team lists a title, high-level summary, demo link, final project link, detailed description, NASA and other data resources, AI use disclosure, and acceptance of the participant terms as submission fields. [Global Organizing Team submission guidance](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1858)
- If the team is registered in Tashkent, the posted local schedule begins preliminary judging at 14:00 on Sunday, November 15, with final judging at 17:00. The local listing gives times for judging and provides Local Lead contact details for the pitch format. [Tashkent local schedule](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/tashkent/?tab=schedule)
- The 2026 Poznań listing describes a seven-slide presentation **or** a 30-second demonstration video. A November 12 response on Space Apps Connect describes the same alternatives and asks for a publicly accessible demo link. The central Project Submission Guide could not be retrieved on November 14. Check the Project tab for the accepted format and your Local Event for its handoff deadline. [Poznań event instructions](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/local-events/poznan/) · [Space Apps Connect discussion](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1973) · [Central Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/)

## Challenge selection and eligibility

Select **Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map** if the team has chosen to enter ARES Navigator. The brief asks for an integrated, layered Mars location or route view that uses data from multiple NASA science missions and helps an explorer plan a Marswalk and its science stops. The Jezero demo illustrates that planning workflow. The prototype uses archived products and models hypothetical traverses, so describe it as a pre-mission planning exercise. [Official Space Apps challenge listing](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/interplanetary-survival-guide-martian-map/?tab=teams) · [Challenge brief reproduced by HackAthlone](https://hackathlone.com/challenges/interplanetary-survival-guide-martian-map)

## Team actions

- [ ] Confirm registration for each of the four human team members and their appearance on the team page. Confirm the registered Local Event and challenge.
- [ ] Document which components came from Ares Traverse and which were developed during the event. The Global Organizing Team says project work should start at 09:00 local time on November 14 and early work can affect eligibility. Ask the Local Lead how the pre-existing prototype should be treated before making a submission attestation. [Global Organizing Team guidance](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1827) · [Participant terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/)
- [ ] Run the final build on the judging machine. Check planet rotation, switching views, local terrain, elevation provenance, route comparison, and report export. Record the actual region and observed values if any appear on a slide.
- [ ] Prepare the chosen demonstration format. Use the [30-second storyboard and live script](demo.md) or the [seven-slide Field Demo PowerPoint](ARES%20Navigator%20Field%20Demo.pptx). The [slide outline](slides.md) accompanies the deck.
- [ ] Fill the Project tab from [project copy](project-copy.md). Include implemented data, code, imagery, tools, and the [AI usage disclosure](../docs/ai-usage.md). Global Awards require use of a NASA data source from the chosen challenge's Resources list. The challenge mirror lists Mars Trek and the Perseverance imagery gallery among its resources. Check the sources actually integrated into the build against the production Resources tab. [Global Organizing Team guidance](https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps/discussions/1827) · [Mirrored challenge resources](https://hackathlone.com/challenges/interplanetary-survival-guide-martian-map)
- [ ] Add the actual source and demo links. Test any link intended for judges in a signed-out browser. Use a URL the judges can open with the required access settings.
- [ ] Complete the Project tab and submit by the applicable deadline. Confirm that the submission is recorded and the links open from the submitted page.
